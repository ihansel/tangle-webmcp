# Video production guide

## Capture

- Record at 1920 × 1080 or 2560 × 1440, 16:9, 30 fps.
- Use 90–100% browser zoom and confirm all node labels are legible at 1080p.
- Keep the cursor visible, move deliberately, and stop moving while explaining.
- Hide bookmarks, profile photos, notifications, unrelated tabs, desktop icons,
  personal data, API keys, credentials, and browser-extension UI.
- Use the dedicated synthetic pipelines only. Do not show Sites access settings
  or private account details.
- Prepare churn and clustering reports in separate tabs before recording.
- Rehearse the permission refusal so the first recorded run is not accidentally
  pre-authorized.

## Audio

- Record narration separately after the screen edit for consistent pacing and
  easy retakes.
- Place a cardioid microphone 15–20 cm from the speaker, slightly off axis, with
  a pop filter if available.
- Close windows/fans and capture 10 seconds of room tone.
- Aim for voice peaks around -6 dBFS and average speech around -16 to -14 LUFS;
  avoid clipping.
- Use light noise reduction and compression only; prioritize natural, clear
  speech.
- No background music is needed. If music is added, use only a track with
  demonstrable commercial/public-video rights, save the licence evidence, mix it
  at least 18 dB below narration, and credit it as required.

## Editing

- Remove loading waits, cursor hunting, repeated prompts, and dead air.
- Use restrained 110–125% digital zooms only for the permission control, a tool
  invocation, and key report metrics.
- Use at most three short callouts: “10 structured tools,” “one-run permission,”
  and “runs in a Web Worker.”
- Keep every claim synchronized with visible evidence.
- Add accurate burned-in or YouTube captions and manually correct WebMCP,
  Product2Vec, Tangle, and model names.
- Do not add unlicensed footage, fonts, music, logos, screenshots, or trademarks.
- Keep the final runtime at or below 2:59; target 2:38.

## Export for YouTube

- MP4 container, H.264 High Profile, progressive, 1920 × 1080.
- 30 fps, constant frame rate.
- 12–20 Mbps video bitrate (VBR), AAC-LC stereo, 48 kHz, 256–320 kbps.
- Rec.709 color; check dark UI details on a normal laptop display.
- Watch the exported file from start to finish before upload.
- Upload as Public or Unlisted only if the rules explicitly accept Unlisted;
  otherwise use Public.
- After upload, open the URL in a private/incognito window, confirm audio,
  captions, 1080p playback, visibility, and runtime below three minutes.
