# Upstream and attribution

This project is a hackathon fork of
[TangleML/tangle-ui](https://github.com/TangleML/tangle-ui), licensed under the
Apache License 2.0.

The fork baseline is upstream commit
`d7768e85d515ac824adec6dabd1f3f7f88f1cf2e` from 1 September 2026. The original
`LICENSE`, source history, product branding, and source attribution are
preserved.

Hackathon additions are concentrated under `src/webmcp/`, plus the local
equipment-failure fixture and related documentation/tests. They add an open
WebMCP adapter and a deliberately narrow browser-native classical ML runner;
they do not claim that all upstream Tangle components can execute locally.
